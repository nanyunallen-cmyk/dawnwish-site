Privacy

Your wishes stay with you

Dawnwish has no account, no server, no ads and no analytics. What you write stays on your iPhone.

Effective 31 October 2026

What Dawnwish keeps

Dawnwish keeps your wishes, the dates you wrote them, which ones came true, your flowers and garden, and your settings (such as your reminder time and Calmer motion). All of this is stored in a small database inside the app on your iPhone.

None of it is sent to us or to anyone else. We cannot see it, and we have no copy of it.

What Dawnwish does not do

  • No account or sign-in. We never ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • No ads and no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics, tracking or usage measurement inside the app.
  • No in-app purchases.
  • We do not sell, rent or share any information, because we do not collect any.

Morning notes

If you ask for a morning reminder, your iPhone schedules it on the phone itself. No server is involved, and the reminder never contains your wishes.

Face ID

If you turn on Lock with Face ID, Dawnwish asks iOS to check that it is you. Apple handles Face ID; Dawnwish never sees or stores your face data.

A gentle check on heavy wishes

Before a wish rises, Dawnwish looks for a few words that may mean you are going through something hard, so it can show support resources. This check happens entirely on your iPhone. Your wish is not sent anywhere and no one is told.

If you choose a support option, your iPhone opens the Phone or Messages app, or the website findahelpline.com. What you share there is between you and that service, under their own privacy terms.

When you share or export

  • Share as a card makes a picture of your pressed flower, its name and the month. It never includes your wish text.
  • Export my wishes makes a file of your wishes and opens the iPhone share sheet. You choose where it goes, and that app's privacy terms then apply.

Backups

If iCloud Backup is on for your iPhone, Apple's backup may include Dawnwish's data, as it does for most apps. That backup is held by Apple under your Apple account, not by us.

Crash reports from Apple

If you agreed in iPhone Settings to share analytics with app developers, or you send feedback through TestFlight, Apple may pass us crash reports or your feedback. These reports describe what the app was doing when it crashed. Dawnwish does not add your wishes to them.

Deleting your data

Deleting the Dawnwish app from your iPhone deletes everything it kept. Because we hold no copy, we cannot restore it afterwards. If you want to keep your words, use Export my wishes first.

Children

Dawnwish is not made for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect anything from anyone, of any age.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above. If Dawnwish ever starts to send any information off your phone, we will say so here and in the app before it happens.
