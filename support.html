Support

How can we help?

Write to dawnwish.support@gmail.com. We read every message and usually reply within a few days.

Please don't include your wishes in your message. We never need them to help you.

Where are my wishes kept?

Only on your iPhone. There is no account and no server, so we cannot see, recover or restore them. Read more in the privacy policy.

Can I move my wishes to a new iPhone?

Yes, when you set up the new iPhone from an iCloud or computer backup of the old one. You can also keep your own copy at any time: Settings in Dawnwish, then Export my wishes.

My morning note doesn't arrive

Open the iPhone Settings app, then Notifications, then Dawnwish, and check that Allow Notifications is on. Focus modes can also hold notes back. If you already wrote a wish that morning, Dawnwish skips the note on purpose.

Face ID doesn't open Dawnwish

Choose Use passcode on the lock screen to open it with your iPhone passcode. You can turn the lock off in Dawnwish's Settings.

The flower and sunrise look still

That happens when Calmer motion is on in Dawnwish's Settings, or Reduce Motion is on in the iPhone's Accessibility settings. Everything still works; it just moves less.

I missed a few mornings

That's all right. Nothing wilts and nothing is lost. Your flower will wait.

How do I delete everything?

Delete the Dawnwish app from your iPhone. This removes all your wishes and flowers for good, so export them first if you'd like to keep them.

If things feel heavy

Dawnwish is not a crisis service, and we cannot reply quickly. If you are in danger right now, please call 911. In the US you can call or text 988, or text HOME to 741741. Outside the US, findahelpline.com lists free helplines. In the app, Settings, then If things feel heavy, shows these at any time.
