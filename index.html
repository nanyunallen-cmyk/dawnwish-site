Dawnwish

One wish, tended each morning.

Plant a flower by writing one wish that matters to you. Each morning, tend it in one small way: water it, give it light, or say a word. Over about two weeks it grows from seed to bud, then rests for as long as your wish takes.

When your wish comes true, it blooms, and you press it into your garden. Missing a day costs nothing; your flower will wait.

No account, no ads, no streaks. Your wish stays on your iPhone.
